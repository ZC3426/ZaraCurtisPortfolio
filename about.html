About Me

I am a curious and empathy-driven product designer, passionate about psychology, philosophy and the relationships humans forge with ourselves, others, and the world around us.

My passion for product and product design stems from my unwavering commitment to improve the lives of others using my skills in empathy, critical thinking and resilience. I first applied these skills in an academic context to improve the lives of young and old learners alike. I now am seeking to apply myself to improve financial and educational products.

My mixed and multi-everything background has given me the gift of comfort in discomfort, making me highly adaptable and light on my feet during times of change and stress. I have never experienced the privilege of feeling like I belonged, or was a part of a greater cultural context. This lifelong challenge instilled in me a deep and profound sense of curiosity, which I have nurtured and continue to nurture through personal and professional projects. It is a gift to not feel burdened by a ‘norm’, and professionally, frees space to explore avenues left untouched. I am excited to get stuck into projects that make life easier, more comprehendible, or more enjoyable for people. If this sounds like something you care about as well, do reach out and connect!
