About Me

Psychology graduate, now solving problems through design.

I'm a BSc Psychology graduate with experience creating digital experiences for diverse audiences, generating inclusive and empathy-driven solutions, and conducting primary and secondary research and behavioural analyses.

My interest in UX comes from a need to solve problems, a curiosity about people, and a desire to make the complex simple. As a mixed person in every sense of the phrase, I've grown deeply curious about the perspectives and experiences of others — I had to learn to put myself in someone else's shoes to bridge gaps where I shared little in common racially, culturally, or linguistically. That habit of reflection is what led me to study psychology and research, and to seek out roles where I could use empathy and evidence together to solve real people-problems: untangling a student's complex situation, getting inside a client's head, or thinking critically about a participant's experience to build the most accurate, most helpful outcome for the end user.

I'm currently looking for a UX Design or UX Research role within a collaborative team that values growth, innovation, and evidence-based design — somewhere I can keep learning while contributing meaningfully.
